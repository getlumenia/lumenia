# lumen-drop-near: the NEAR escrow of Lumenia (send USDC by link, claim walletless through a function-call key, unclaimed money home by itself through a NEP-519 return timer; on testnet as lumenia-near.testnet and on mainnet as lumenia.near; not audited)
A Cargo workspace of its own inside the monorepo: `contract/` is the escrow crate (`lumenia-drop`, deployed as `lumenia-near.testnet`), `test-usdc/` and `test-root/` are the sandbox mocks, `scripts/` drives testnet. Everything about the contract (interface, flows, security model, measured gas, tests, deploy history) is in [contract/README.md](contract/README.md); from the monorepo root `pnpm near:verify:offline --only contract` runs its tests, and CI runs them with clippy in the `near` job.
